Good to know

Straight answers.

read me.txt
Do I need both apps?

No. Glidr works alone over Bluetooth, with nothing required on the PC. Glidr Screen is separate: it needs its phone app and the Windows app.

Does it work on mobile data?

Often not out of the box. Carrier networks can block a direct connection. The PC app can ask your router to open a port (UPnP, which only helps if the router has a public address), or you can add your own TURN relay in its settings. With a relay the media stays end-to-end encrypted but passes through that provider.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.

The PC apps are not code-signed yet, so Windows warns on first run. You can read and build everything from the source on GitHub.

Can it control the lock screen or admin windows?

Windows blocks capture and input for the lock screen, UAC prompts and elevated windows unless the PC app runs as administrator.

Is there a view-only mode?

Yes. Turn off Allow remote control in the PC app and the phone shows a "View only" tag. A separate Sharing switch pauses all connections.

Does the optional Glidr PC companion need anything?

It needs the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime. It only adds now-playing info; the mouse and keyboard work without it.
